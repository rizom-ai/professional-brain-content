# Plan — Upwork as a fast-income channel (assisted, human-approved)

**Goal:** first paid contract within 1–3 weeks. Not premium positioning — cash flow.
**Constraint:** Upwork's ToS forbids automated proposal submission. Ban = channel dead.
**So:** automate triage + drafting. The send is always a human click.

Companion to `plattform-setup-pack-freelance-profile-dachnl-freelancermap-malt-freelancenl-linkedin` (platform setup), `freelance-positioning-dachnl-profil-raten-launch-checkliste` (profile texts) and `freelance-strategie-intern-dr-jan-hein-hoogstad` (rates, legal/tax). Phase 4 reuses the inbound-triage plugin plan wholesale.

---

## 0. Why this channel

| | freelancermap / Malt | Upwork |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | wait to be found | bid — you can force it |
| First contract | 4–6 weeks | 1–3 weeks |
| Rate | €105/h listed | $70–90 cold start → $130+ after reviews |
| August effect | dead (EU holidays) | none (global) |
| Rate isolation | public, anchors future deals | invisible to EU recruiters |

Rate isolation is the key unlock: a low first contract here costs nothing on the EU channels. That's what makes cold-start pricing acceptable — **if** it stays time-boxed.

## 2. Rate ladder (Upwork-only, time-boxed)

| Stage | Trigger | Hourly | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold start | contracts 1–3 | **$80–90** | buys reviews + JSS, nothing else |
| Established | 3–5 reviews, JSS ≥ 90 % | **$110–130** | raise immediately, not gradually |
| Top Rated | badge earned | **$150+** | AI work supports this |
| Floor | never below | **$70** | ≈ $63 net after fee — below this, don't bother |

Fixed-price (Catalog): audit **$1.500** · build **$3.000**.
Consultations: **$150–200/h** — full rate from day one, no discount.

**Raise trigger is a hard rule.** The cold-start rate is a loss-leader for reviews. If it is still $85 at contract #6, the plan has failed.

## 3. Passive layer (works without a track record)

Set up before any bidding — these get bought without you applying:

1. **Consultations** — 60 min, $150–200. *"AI agent architecture review — will your approach survive production?"* Low-risk yes for the client, full rate for you, and **each booking generates a review**. Fastest route out of the cold start; sells judgment, not hours.
2. **Project Catalog** (2–3 offerings, fixed price — also avoids the screenshot time tracker):
   - LLM agent architecture audit — $1.500
   - Self-hosted RAG / knowledge base deployment — $3.000
   - Codebase rescue assessment (take-over readiness) — $1.500
3. **Availability badge on**, profile public, response rate high — all feed search ranking.

## 4. Fit filter (the triage rules — codify these)

**Hard exclude**
- Payment method unverified
- Client 0 hires with a large budget, or hire rate < 50 % (tire kickers)
- Hourly < $70 · fixed-price < $500 for multi-week scope
- "Full-time, 40 h/week, long-term" — capacity is 15–20 h
- > 30 bids already, unless posted < 1 h ago
- One-paragraph spec with no domain detail (scope creep guaranteed)

**Prefer**
- AI agents / LLM / RAG / MCP · architecture review · technical due diligence · fractional CTO · rescue of a half-finished codebase
- TypeScript / Node / Bun / Elixir / Clojure
- **Posted < 4 h ago** — response speed is the single biggest win-rate lever
- Client lifetime spend > $5k
- Founder/startup buyer (decides fast) over enterprise procurement

## 5. Proposal anatomy (what actually wins)

Max ~150 words. The client sees **only the first two lines** in the list — they carry it.

1. **Hook (2 lines):** their problem in their words. No "I am writing to express interest."
2. **One proof point**, matched to their problem — not a CV dump:
   - agents/LLM → brains (production, self-hosted, MCP + OAuth)
   - scaling → Snowday (two-sided platform, NYC)
   - public-sector/standards → Public Badges
   - knowledge/RAG → Lefthoek, Rizom
3. **One concrete question** about their stack or constraint — invites a reply, proves you read it.
4. **Availability + rate**, one line.

Tailored beats volume decisively here: Upwork penalises spam patterns, and clients spot generic proposals instantly. 5 good proposals > 30 templated ones.

## 7. Metrics + kill criteria

Track weekly: proposals sent · viewed · replied · interviews · contracts · Connects spent.

- Reply rate **5–15 %** is normal cold-start. Below 5 % after 20 proposals → positioning or rate problem, not a volume problem. Stop and re-diagnose; do not bid harder.
- **30 proposals, 0 replies → halt.** Rewrite positioning before spending more Connects.
- First contract by week 3 = on track. Nothing by week 4 → Upwork is not the channel; shift to APAC agencies.

## 8. Risks

- **ToS / ban** — never auto-send. Human click, always. This is not negotiable.
- **Cold-start rate becoming permanent** — mitigated by the hard raise trigger (§2).
- **Location bias** — profile shows Taiwan; some clients price by geography. Offset with PhD, English, Dutch entity. Real friction, not fatal.
- **Scope creep** — prefer fixed-price for unknown clients; milestones always.
- **Connects burn** — cap weekly spend; §7 kill criteria prevents throwing money at a broken pitch.
- **Tax/entity** — W-8BEN-E, BV as contracting party; Taiwan residency questions already open in `freelance-strategie-intern-dr-jan-hein-hoogstad`.
