# Expert Networks — Channel Setup

Drafted 2026-08-04. A new income channel: paid hour-long calls with investors and corporates researching AI infrastructure. The fastest path from nothing to an invoice — asynchronous signup, no proposals to write, near-zero delivery risk.

## Why this channel

Clients pay roughly $900–1,500 per hour; experts typically start at $300–500 with room above that for niche technical expertise.

The networks recruit mainly by **searching LinkedIn**, not by processing inbound applications. Being findable matters as much as signing up.

What they are being asked for right now is exactly the subject matter: how organisations actually deploy LLM agents, self-hosted versus vendor-hosted infrastructure, MCP and open agent standards, why most pilots stall.

## Where to apply

GLG, AlphaSights, Third Bridge, Guidepoint, Dialectica. About ten minutes each, and they run asynchronously in the background while other work continues.

**Starting rate: $400/hour.** Below that reads as junior. Trivially raised after a few calls.

**Compliance.** These networks screen hard on client confidentiality. The Datacampus and Port of Rotterdam conversations are off-limits as call material.

**LinkedIn.** The headline is currently the English Rizom-first prose line (since 2026-08-06); the expert-network search terms — AI agents, self-hosted, founder and CTO, Ph.D. — are carried in natural prose in headline and About.
