## The gap

Every company in your portfolio is improvising AI separately. Each one is
re-deciding the same build-versus-buy questions, re-vetting the same vendors,
and re-learning the same lessons — and none of them can afford the senior
seat that should own those decisions. The result is a portfolio where AI
progress is invisible, uneven, and impossible to compare.

## The program

A fixed-scope engagement per company, run across **three to four portfolio
companies per quarter** — one buyer, one invoice, comparable results.

Each company gets the sprint (4–6 weeks):

- **An AI audit at due-diligence depth** — where AI actually earns its place
  in the product and the org, what to build versus buy, and where AI
  shouldn't be used at all.
- **A team brain, deployed and seeded** — open-source agent infrastructure
  grown from the company's actual knowledge: shared memory that outlasts
  departures, searchable by meaning, running on their own servers.
- **An upskilled team** — the engineers they already have, made AI-capable;
  no parallel AI team to hire.

The fund gets what no single engagement can produce: a **cross-portfolio AI
readiness picture** — the same audit lens applied across companies, so you
can see who is ahead, who is stuck, and where the next dollar of support
actually lands.

## What each company keeps

Everything. The brain is open source, runs on their infrastructure, and
holds what their team learned — it keeps working after we leave. No
licenses, no lock-in, no consulting theater: the deliverable is a running
system, not a deck.
