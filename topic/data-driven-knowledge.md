---
title: Data-Driven Knowledge
---
Data-driven knowledge systems examine how data, computation, archives, and digital tools reshape the production, evaluation, and application of knowledge. They challenge the idea that large datasets offer direct access to reality, emphasizing that data is interpreted through technical, disciplinary, organizational, artistic, and commercial practices. Effective systems treat archives and information resources as active infrastructures requiring recurring audits to identify gaps, inconsistencies, accessibility problems, and weaknesses. By combining critical reflection on agency, representation, and human control with systematic evaluation of data quality, these systems can support creative research, stronger analysis, and more reliable decision-making. Keywords: data-driven knowledge, computational research, knowledge systems, digital archives, data quality, archive audits, organizational decision-making, data governance, interdisciplinary research.
