---
title: Knowledge Tool Design
---
Knowledge tool design is the development of digital systems that transform scattered information into durable, context-aware knowledge for interpretation, decision-making, collaboration, and organizational learning. These systems support knowledge capture, preservation, organization, semantic structuring, retrieval, citation, and application through practical workflows rather than information accumulation alone. Effective tools preserve institutional memory and the context needed to evaluate sources, connect knowledge to action, and support responsible collaboration, including work involving AI agents. Design should align with domain practices and be reviewed by technical, information, and subject-matter experts. Evaluation should examine knowledge gaps, contradictions, usability, navigation, retrieval quality, context preservation, and whether improved access helps users make better decisions and perform important organizational work. Keywords: knowledge infrastructure, information architecture, information retrieval, knowledge capture, knowledge preservation, semantic markup, metadata, citation management, institutional memory, organizational learning, collaborative tools, AI-ready information, knowledge evaluation
