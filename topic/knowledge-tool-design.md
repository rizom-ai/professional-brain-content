---
title: Knowledge Tool Design
---
AI-ready knowledge infrastructure is the design of extensible, self-hosted systems that transform scattered information into durable, context-aware knowledge for retrieval, interpretation, decision-making, collaboration, and organizational learning. These systems support knowledge capture, preservation, semantic structuring, metadata, citation, source evaluation, and practical application while maintaining institutional memory and the context needed for responsible use. Key capabilities may include AI agents, LLM integration, Model Context Protocol support, plugin architectures, deployment tooling, and user ownership of data. Effective infrastructure aligns with domain practices, supports human and machine collaboration, and is evaluated through knowledge coverage, contradiction handling, retrieval quality, usability, navigation, context preservation, and its impact on important organizational work. Keywords: knowledge infrastructure, AI agents, LLM integration, Model Context Protocol, self-hosted systems, information architecture, knowledge capture, semantic markup, metadata, citation management, institutional memory, organizational learning, plugin architecture, knowledge retrieval, knowledge evaluation
