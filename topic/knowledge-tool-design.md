---
title: Knowledge Tool Design
---
Knowledge tool design is the development of digital systems that transform scattered information into usable, durable knowledge for interpretation, decision-making, collaboration, and organizational learning. These systems support knowledge capture, preservation, organization, retrieval, contextual understanding, and application through information architecture, semantic structure, metadata, citation management, and practical workflows. They strengthen institutional memory and context-aware collaboration, including work involving AI agents, while preserving the context needed to evaluate sources and act responsibly. Effective design requires alignment with domain practices and review by technical, information, and subject-matter experts. Evaluation should address knowledge gaps, contradictions, usability, navigation, retrieval quality, and the impact of tools on important organizational work. Keywords: knowledge infrastructure, information architecture, information retrieval, knowledge capture, knowledge preservation, semantic markup, metadata, citation management, institutional memory, organizational learning, collaborative tools, AI-ready information, knowledge evaluation
