---
title: 'Portable, Federated Knowledge Infrastructure'
---
Open-source, interoperable infrastructure that keeps personal, organizational, and institutional knowledge under its owners’ control while enabling movement across SaaS platforms, self-hosted repositories, local tools, and collaborative networks. It supports complete, machine-readable exports; importers and exporters; bidirectional synchronization; portable data models; citations, relationships, metadata, and provenance; and round-trip conformance testing with transparent loss reports showing what survives migration. Independently owned knowledge spaces can federate through selective, consent-based sharing, discovery, and citation without surrendering local authority or requiring dependence on a central platform. Core capabilities include self-hosting, data ownership, practical exit rights, registries, federated search, archive preservation, semantic metadata, open lexicons, extensible plugins, and accountable AI-agent architectures. Trustworthy implementations emphasize transparent licensing, privacy protections, human oversight, correction and reversal mechanisms, inspectable methods, and plural community governance. The domain applies across education, media, culture, health, government, and organizational settings, supporting institutional memory, collective archives, collaborative learning, public-interest repositories, digital autonomy, and durable knowledge commons while resisting platform capture and vendor dependence.
