---
title: Digital Trust
---
Digital trust is the foundation for digital systems and AI agents whose claims, decisions, and actions can be understood, traced, verified, attributed, and challenged. It combines cybersecurity, privacy, transparent data practices, accountable governance, dependable operations, and credible evidence. In AI-mediated systems, trust requires provenance trails showing which sources, models, agents, and processes shaped an output; clearly assigned roles and bounded authority; documented deployment and evaluation practices; escalation mechanisms; and enforceable contestation rights for people affected by harmful, unjustified, or erroneous decisions. Public documentation, open research, inspectable records, references, project links, ratings, reviews, and trust badges can strengthen credibility across digital platforms, professional services, technology partnerships, education, research, and organizational AI. Digital trust depends on consistent behavior, transparent structures, verifiable evidence, decision traceability, and clear responsibility—not unsupported assertions, frictionless automation, or opaque algorithms.
