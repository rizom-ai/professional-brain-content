---
title: Ecosystem Architecture
---
Ecosystem architecture is presented as a practice for designing organizations that function more like living systems than machines. It emphasizes cultivation, adaptability, relationships, and contexts rather than rigid hierarchy or fixed organizational structures. The concept draws from philosophy, Afrofuturism, Shanzhai culture, DAOs, and mesh networks. It responds to the limits of traditional organizational models in contemporary digital environments. The goal is to create organizations that are inhabitable, resilient, and capable of supporting meaningful participation. This framework is the central intellectual and practical focus of the profile.
