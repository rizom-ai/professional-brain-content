---
title: Ecosystem Architecture
---
Ecosystem architecture is the practice of designing adaptable organizational ecosystems that connect people, institutions, technologies, expertise, and values-based missions. Rather than relying on rigid hierarchies, it treats organizations as living infrastructures shaped by relationships, distributed coordination, informal knowledge, organizational memory, and shared context. The practice includes partnership design, network formation, matchmaking systems, institutional strategy, and technical architecture. Its aim is to create inhabitable, resilient systems in which distributed actors can participate meaningfully and scale shared initiatives. Relevant perspectives include Afrofuturism, Shanzhai culture, DAOs, mesh networks, and other models of decentralized collaboration. Keywords: organizational ecosystems, network design, distributed coordination, partnership development, institutional infrastructure, organizational memory, technical architecture, resilient organizations, decentralized collaboration.
