title: Software Architecture
---
Software architecture is presented as an end-to-end discipline spanning initial design decisions, product development, infrastructure, deployment, and ongoing operation. The profile highlights more than two decades of experience building complete products as a founder, CTO, and senior architect. A recurring concern is creating infrastructure that can be managed by an individual while remaining scalable for a larger team. The content also stresses ownership of outcomes rather than completing isolated tickets. Stabilizing and extending inherited codebases is another important architectural capability described in the profile.
