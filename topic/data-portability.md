---
title: Data Portability
---
A reusable standard for keeping organisational and personal knowledge under its owner’s control while moving it between SaaS platforms, self-hosted repositories, tools such as Obsidian and Git, private knowledge bases, and shared collaborative spaces. It should preserve essential content, metadata, relationships, provenance, ownership, and consent controls through complete export formats, reference exporters and importers, bidirectional synchronization, and round-trip conformance tests. Transparent loss reports must show which information survives migration and what is lost. Portability should also support open federation and selective, consent-based sharing between independently owned knowledge spaces, allowing owners to control which entities become visible in shared learning networks without surrendering authority over their data. This makes portability a foundation for interoperability, user autonomy, and sustainable collaboration rather than merely a technical export feature.  Keywords: data portability, federated knowledge, export formats, importers, bidirectional synchronization, metadata, provenance, round-trip conformance, interoperability, self-hosting, selective sharing, consent management, data ownership, open federation
