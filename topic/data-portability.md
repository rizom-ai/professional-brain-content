---
title: Data Portability
---
A reusable standard for proving that organisational and personal knowledge can move between SaaS platforms, self-hosted repositories, private knowledge bases, and shared collaborative spaces without losing essential content, metadata, relationships, provenance, ownership, or consent controls. The standard should define a complete export format and support reference exporters, importers, bidirectional synchronization, and round-trip conformance tests that identify what survives migration and what is lost. Loss reports should provide transparent evidence rather than conceal implementation gaps. Portability must also enable selective sharing, allowing owners to control which entities become visible to collaborators while retaining authority over their data. Keywords: data portability, AI knowledge platforms, export and import, metadata, provenance, round-trip testing, interoperability, self-hosting, synchronization, selective sharing, consent, data ownership.
