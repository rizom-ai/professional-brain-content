---
title: Data Portability
---
The proposal focuses on making data portability verifiable for AI-powered knowledge platforms. It argues that conventional export features rarely prove that organisational content, metadata, relationships, and provenance can be transferred completely. The proposed standard defines what a complete export should contain in a reusable format. A reference exporter, importer, and round-trip conformance test would reveal what survives migration and what is lost. Loss reports are treated as valuable evidence rather than hidden implementation failures. The goal is to give organisations a demonstrable ability to leave self-hosted or SaaS infrastructure without losing institutional memory.
