---
title: Research Funding
---
A systematic approach to building and managing a diversified funding pipeline for interdisciplinary research, digital culture, artificial intelligence, governance, and open infrastructure. It separates durable research and infrastructure lanes, such as institutional responsibility research, AI governance, open-source software, the brains repository, and situated-agent standards, while matching projects to grants, fellowships, philanthropic programs, public schemes, and open-source funding. Effective practice includes reusable proposal assets—problem statements, methods, white papers, writing samples, videos, artifacts, and researcher profiles—adapted to each funder. Opportunities are assessed by eligibility, thematic fit, funding amount, deadlines, application status, co-financing, residency, partnerships, organizational vehicles, and administrative constraints. Related proposals may be distributed across individual and organizational applicants when deliverables remain distinct, transparent, and non-duplicative. The pipeline should also document unsuitable, closed, or poorly aligned opportunities to prevent repeated effort and improve future funder matching.[
