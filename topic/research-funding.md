---
title: Research Funding
---
A systematic approach to identifying, evaluating, pursuing, and managing diversified funding for interdisciplinary research and build-oriented infrastructure. It covers grants, fellowships, public programs, philanthropy, residencies, partnerships, open calls, and open-source funding, with opportunities matched to clearly defined projects, applicant structures, partnership models, budgets, deadlines, eligibility rules, co-financing requirements, and administrative constraints. Core practices include distinguishing research proposals from infrastructure initiatives, separating project scopes to avoid duplicate claims or funding-cap issues, adapting reusable assets such as proposals, methods, white papers, CVs, writing samples, scripts, videos, and artifacts to each funder, and maintaining a pipeline of active, prospective, unsuitable, and closed opportunities. Relevant domains include institutional responsibility, AI governance, digital culture, open-source software, situated-agent standards, organized memory, and shared infrastructure.
