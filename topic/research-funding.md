---
title: Research Funding
---
A systematic approach to building and managing a diversified funding pipeline for interdisciplinary research, digital culture, artificial intelligence, and open infrastructure. It covers grants, fellowships, philanthropic programs, public schemes, and open-source funding; separates research and infrastructure lanes; and matches funders to projects involving institutional responsibility, situated-agent standards, and open-source implementations. Effective practice includes modular proposal assets such as problem statements, methods, white papers, writing samples, artifacts, and researcher profiles; evaluation of eligibility, funding size, deadlines, thematic fit, co-financing, residency, organizational vehicles, partnerships, and administrative requirements; and tracking unsuitable or closed opportunities. Applications should be adapted to each funder while distinguishing related work—such as software implementation versus standards development—to avoid duplication. Keywords: grants, fellowships, philanthropy, public funding, open-source funding, research pipeline, proposal development, funder matching, eligibility, deadlines, co-financing, partnerships, research infrastructure, artificial intelligence, digital culture, open-source software, standards, situated agents.
