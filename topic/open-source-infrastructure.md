---
title: Open Source Infrastructure
---
Open-source knowledge infrastructure provides reusable, inspectable, and community-owned foundations for creating, connecting, preserving, and coordinating collective knowledge. It emphasizes self-hosting, data ownership, institutional memory, and independence from centralized platforms. Core elements include plain-text and open-licensed knowledge bases, signal models, open lexicons, interoperable references such as the AT Protocol, registries, citation and discovery mechanisms, plugin architectures, and AI-assisted coordination. Implementations may use technologies such as TypeScript, Bun, MCP, and deployment tooling, with AGPL licensing and public repositories supporting transparency and reuse. The domain spans institutional knowledge systems, collaborative learning, public-interest technology, and accountable infrastructure for education, media, culture, health, public-sector, and organizational use. Keywords: open source, knowledge infrastructure, self-hosting, data ownership, institutional memory, open knowledge bases, AT Protocol, interoperability, open lexicon, signal model, community ownership, AGPL, AI coordination, public-interest technology, plugin architecture
