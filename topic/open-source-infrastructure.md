---
title: Open Source Infrastructure
---
Open-source infrastructure encompasses the design and development of reusable, inspectable, and community-adaptable technological foundations. It includes Rizom’s self-hosted AI and institutional knowledge infrastructure, built with TypeScript, Bun, MCP, plugin architecture, and deployment tooling; the brains repository; and research focused on data ownership, institutional memory, and independence from vendor-hosted services. It also reflects sustained open-source practice through Offcourse, an open platform for crowdlearning and distributed knowledge sharing, including AI-assisted coordination, as well as the Public Badges framework for assessing websites and software against public-interest values. Across full-stack development, DevOps, system architecture, and polyglot programming, these projects demonstrate a commitment to accountable infrastructure for education, media, culture, health, public-sector adoption, and organizational coordination, supported by clearly organized public repositories on GitHub and GitLab. Keywords: open source, self-hosting, AI infrastructure, institutional knowledge, collaborative learning, data ownership, public-interest technology, knowledge coordination, plugin architecture, DevOps, system architecture, public-sector technology
