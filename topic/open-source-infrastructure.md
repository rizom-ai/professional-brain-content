---
title: Open Source Infrastructure
---
Open-source infrastructure is the foundation of a professional and public-interest technology practice focused on institutional knowledge, collaborative learning, and shared ownership. Rizom demonstrates this through self-hosted AI infrastructure built with TypeScript, Bun, MCP, plugin architecture, and deployment tooling, emphasizing data ownership, institutional memory, and independence from vendor-hosted services. The broader body of work includes eleven years of open-source development at Offcourse, an open platform for crowdlearning and distributed knowledge sharing, and the Public Badges framework for evaluating websites and software against public-interest values. GitHub and GitLab repositories serve as public evidence of sustained technical practice and should be clearly organized and connected to the broader professional identity. Together, these projects show expertise in building accountable, reusable infrastructure for education, media, culture, health, public-sector adoption, and organizational coordination. Keywords: open source, self-hosting, AI infrastructure, institutional knowledge, collaborative learning, data ownership, public-interest technology, knowledge coordination, plugin architecture, public-sector technology.
