---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI is the design and deployment of language models and autonomous agents as accountable participants in social, organizational, and institutional environments rather than isolated software components. The domain combines open-source infrastructure, reference implementations, deployment practices, technical specifications, and standards for context-aware agents. Key concerns include organizational roles, shared memory, knowledge provenance, interoperability, access boundaries, human agency, collaboration patterns, institutional values, and independent oversight. Production-ready systems should make agent behavior understandable, repeatable, reviewable, and responsive to fragmented or conflicting records and unclear authority. Applications include organizational knowledge coordination, virtual team members, routine work automation, modular agent networks, and collective knowledge stewardship. Situated AI connects philosophical questions about agency with practical infrastructure for accountable, interoperable, and context-sensitive AI systems. Keywords: embedded AI, autonomous agents, organizational workflows, virtual team members, human-AI collaboration, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, agent accountability, modular agents, institutional context, open-source infrastructure, provenance, interoperability, situated-agent standards
