---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI embeds language models and autonomous agents within social, organizational, and institutional environments, treating them as participants in real practices rather than isolated software features. Applications include organizational knowledge coordination, virtual team members, routine work automation, modular agent networks, and shared-context preservation. Their effectiveness depends on organizational roles, collective memory, collaboration patterns, reliable knowledge infrastructure, access boundaries, human agency, accountability, domain practices, and independent oversight. Production-ready systems should make agent behavior understandable, repeatable, and reviewable while addressing fragmented or conflicting records, unclear authority, institutional values, and the political context of organizational information. Examples such as Rizom, Varias, and Bots With an Attitude illustrate how situated agents can support coordination, knowledge stewardship, team performance, and emergent collective behavior.

Keywords: embedded AI, autonomous agents, organizational workflows, virtual team members, human-AI collaboration, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, agent accountability, modular agents, institutional context
