---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI Systems examines how AI agents and human-AI systems are designed, deployed, and governed within the institutional, organizational, technical, and social contexts where they operate. It covers large language model workflows, autonomous and modular agents, virtual team members, organizational knowledge coordination, and production-ready knowledge infrastructure. The central concern is designing capabilities, roles, authority, access, limitations, interactions, and responsibility so AI functions as an accountable collaborator rather than an isolated automation tool. Architectural patterns and organizational memory can make behavior more understandable, repeatable, and reviewable, while independent oversight remains important for high-stakes decisions. Situated deployment must also address how AI can amplify unclear roles, fragmented archives, unresolved conflicts, weak accountability, and politically situated knowledge that fluent outputs may present as objective. The topic connects agent development, collaborative work, product building, technical architecture, organizational operations, and long-term AI governance in real social settings. Keywords: artificial intelligence, human-AI systems, large language models, autonomous agents, modular agents, virtual team members, organizational memory, knowledge coordination, knowledge infrastructure, system architecture, role design, collaborative AI, AI governance, accountability, institutional context, independent review, production deployment
