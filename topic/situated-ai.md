---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI concerns the design and deployment of AI agents embedded in organizational, social, cultural, and institutional contexts rather than operating as standalone automation. It includes agents that coordinate distributed knowledge, preserve organizational memory, manage information, support modular workflows and virtual teams, and participate in shared human systems. The domain covers modular and interoperable agents, open learning and knowledge infrastructures, retrieval-augmented generation, open standards such as the Model Context Protocol, and self-hosted or vendor-hosted deployment. Reliable systems require adaptation to local context, explicit roles and bounded authority, provenance, traceability, predictable behavior, escalation and contestation procedures, human oversight, and clear responsibility. Emphasis is placed on open, governed, sustainable infrastructure that preserves human autonomy, supports portability and sovereignty, and remains useful beyond individual products or funding cycles. Keywords: situated AI, organizational AI agents, institutional knowledge, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, workflow automation, virtual team members, modular agents, agent interoperability, open standards, Model Context Protocol, retrieval-augmented generation, governed AI, human oversight, provenance, traceability, portability, AI sovereignty, public-sector AI.
