---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI examines how artificial intelligence is shaped by—and reshapes—the institutional, organizational, and technical conditions in which it operates. It covers practical deployment of LLM workflows, autonomous agents, virtual team members, and organizational memory, with emphasis on production-ready knowledge infrastructure that enables systems to access, organize, and reuse institutional knowledge. AI can increase speed and output while also scaling unclear roles, broken archives, unresolved conflicts, and weak accountability. Fluent outputs may make partial or politically situated knowledge appear objective, so responsible use requires explicit and contestable boundaries around access, authority, limitations, and responsibility. The central challenge is role design: building enough organizational memory, context, judgment, and accountability for AI capabilities to function as meaningful and responsible collaborators rather than merely optimizing workflows. These concerns connect AI architecture to concrete product development and operational responsibility, including the design of systems intended for sustained use rather than experimentation alone. Keywords: artificial intelligence, large language models, autonomous agents, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, AI governance, institutional context, role design, accountability, production deployment
