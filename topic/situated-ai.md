---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI systems are designed, deployed, and governed as embedded capabilities within software products, organizations, and working environments. The topic covers large language model workflows, autonomous and modular agents, virtual team members, organizational knowledge coordination, organizational memory, and production-ready knowledge infrastructure. It emphasizes that reliable human-AI collaboration depends on situated knowledge, domain practices, operational constraints, role design, human agency, access boundaries, accountability, and independent oversight. Architectural patterns and organizational memory can make agent behavior more understandable, repeatable, and reviewable, while deployment must also address fragmented archives, unclear authority, unresolved conflicts, and politically situated knowledge that fluent systems may present as objective. Keywords: artificial intelligence, human-AI systems, large language models, autonomous agents, modular agents, virtual team members, situated knowledge, organizational memory, knowledge coordination, knowledge infrastructure, system architecture, role design, human agency, collaborative AI, AI governance, accountability, institutional context, independent review, production deployment
