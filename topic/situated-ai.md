---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI infrastructure concerns the design and deployment of AI agents embedded in organizational, social, cultural, technical, and institutional contexts rather than operating as standalone automation. It encompasses interoperable agents, portable knowledge, organizational memory, distributed coordination, workflow support, virtual teams, retrieval-augmented generation, signal models, lexicons, brains repositories, and open standards such as the Model Context Protocol and AT Protocol. The domain includes both standards and documentation work and reference implementations or deployments, with clear boundaries between personal and organizational projects. Reliable systems require local adaptation, explicit roles and bounded authority, provenance, traceability, predictable behavior, escalation and contestation procedures, human oversight, and clear responsibility. Emphasis is placed on open, governed, sustainable infrastructure that supports portability, sovereignty, public technical learning, and human autonomy beyond individual products or funding cycles. Keywords: situated AI, AI infrastructure, organizational AI agents, institutional knowledge, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, agent interoperability, portable knowledge, workflow automation, virtual teams, open standards, Model Context Protocol, AT Protocol, retrieval-augmented generation, governed AI, human oversight, provenance, traceability, portability, AI sovereignty, public-sector AI
