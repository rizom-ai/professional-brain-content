---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI concerns the design and deployment of AI agents embedded in organizational, educational, social, and cultural contexts. It treats AI deployment as an institutional design problem: agents should have named roles, bounded authority, defined responsibilities, context-aware access, predictable behavior, provenance-carrying outputs, traceable actions, escalation rules, and meaningful human or institutional paths for review and contestation. The approach includes virtual team members, workflow automation, modular agents with interchangeable capabilities, distributed networks, shared knowledge infrastructure, and interoperable open systems. Rather than treating intelligence as isolated, Situated AI emphasizes coordination, organizational memory, human-AI collaboration, collective judgment, autonomy, accountability, and the use of open-source reference implementations to make these requirements implementable, verifiable, and reusable across institutions. Keywords: embedded AI, institutional context, organizational workflows, educational contexts, virtual team members, modular agents, human-AI collaboration, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, bounded authority, provenance, interoperability, escalation rules, contestability, human autonomy, collective judgment, open-source infrastructure
