---
title: Situated AI
---
Governed AI agent infrastructure covers the technical and institutional design of production-grade agents embedded in organizational, educational, public-sector, and regulated environments. It combines self-hosted or vendor-hosted language models, retrieval-augmented generation, organizational knowledge systems, interoperable agent protocols, and modular workflow automation with bounded authority, explicit roles, provenance, traceability, predictable behavior, and documented escalation and contestation procedures. The approach treats deployment as both an engineering and governance problem, addressing pilot-to-production reliability, responsibility laundering, overconfident outputs from limited context, organizational memory, procurement, licensing, sustainability, and human autonomy. Open standards, open-source infrastructure, certified implementation partners, and documented real-world deployments support systems that are inspectable, verifiable, reusable, and accountable.
