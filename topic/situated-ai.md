---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI is the design and deployment of AI agents embedded in organizational, educational, social, and cultural contexts. These agents extend rather than quietly replace human judgment by operating within named roles, bounded authority, explicit responsibilities, context-aware access, predictable behavior, provenance-carrying outputs, and documented escalation and contestation procedures. The approach treats AI deployment as an institutional design problem, addressing risks such as responsibility laundering, excessive confidence from thin context, and the erosion of organizational memory, transactive memory, shared judgment, and human autonomy when institutions outsource decisions to automated systems. It supports accountable virtual team members, workflow automation, modular and interoperable agents, shared knowledge infrastructure, and human-AI collaboration. Open standards, procurement requirements, open-source implementation, published specifications, traceable reasoning, and documented real-world deployments can make these systems inspectable, verifiable, and reusable. Keywords: embedded AI, institutional design, bounded authority, provenance, traceability, escalation, contestation, responsibility laundering, organizational memory, transactive memory, workflow automation, virtual team members, modular agents, interoperability, knowledge infrastructure, human-AI collaboration, open standards, procurement, open-source infrastructure
