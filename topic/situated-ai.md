---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI embeds language models, autonomous agents, and virtual team members within software products, organizations, and working environments. These systems support coordination, repetitive questions, administrative work, and knowledge-intensive workflows by using team context, organizational memory, and production-ready knowledge infrastructure. Reliable deployment depends on clearly defined roles, access boundaries, human agency, accountability, domain practices, operational constraints, and independent oversight. Effective architectures make agent behavior understandable, repeatable, and reviewable while addressing fragmented records, unclear authority, conflicting knowledge, and the political context of organizational information.

Keywords: artificial intelligence, human-AI collaboration, large language models, autonomous agents, modular agents, virtual team members, situated knowledge, organizational memory, knowledge coordination, knowledge infrastructure, workflow automation, system architecture, role design, human agency, AI governance, accountability, institutional context, independent review, production deployment
