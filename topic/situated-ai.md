---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI is the design and deployment of AI agents within specific organizational, social, and cultural contexts. It treats agent deployment as an institutional design problem: each agent should have a named role, bounded authority, defined responsibilities, context-aware access, predictable behavior, provenance-carrying outputs, traceable actions, escalation rules, and meaningful human or institutional paths for review and contestation. This framework addresses responsibility laundering, fragmented organizational memory, and the replacement of institutional judgment by emphasizing human autonomy, accountability, collective evaluation, and human-AI collaboration. It applies to virtual team members, workflow automation, modular agents, distributed networks, shared knowledge infrastructure, and interoperable systems. Open-source reference implementations can make these requirements implementable by engineers, verifiable by procurement officers, and reusable across organizations.

Keywords: embedded AI, institutional context, organizational workflows, virtual team members, modular agents, human-AI collaboration, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, agent accountability, bounded authority, provenance, interoperability, escalation rules, contestability, human autonomy, collective judgment, open-source infrastructure
