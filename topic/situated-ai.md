---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI is the design and deployment of AI agents embedded in organizational, educational, social, and cultural contexts. It treats AI deployment as an institutional design problem, requiring agents to have explicit roles, bounded authority, defined responsibilities, context-aware access, predictable behavior, provenance-carrying outputs, traceable actions, escalation procedures, and meaningful human or institutional paths for review and contestation. This framework addresses responsibility laundering by making accountability assignable and agent behavior governable. It supports virtual team members, workflow automation, modular and interoperable agents, shared knowledge infrastructure, organizational memory, and human-AI collaboration. Open standards, procurement requirements, open-source reference implementations, and documented deployments can make situated-agent practices implementable, verifiable, and reusable across institutions while preserving human autonomy and collective judgment across distributed systems and networks. Keywords: embedded AI, institutional design, organizational roles, bounded authority, accountability, provenance, traceability, escalation procedures, contestation, responsibility laundering, workflow automation, virtual team members, modular agents, interoperability, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, human-AI collaboration, open standards, procurement requirements, open-source implementations
