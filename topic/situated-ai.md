---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI embeds language models and autonomous agents within social, organizational, and institutional environments, treating them as participants in real practices rather than isolated software features. These systems may support focused responsibilities such as coordination, status collection, repetitive questions, administrative work, and preservation of shared context through virtual team members, modular agents, and shared networks. Their effectiveness depends on relationships with users and teams, organizational memory, reliable knowledge infrastructure, clearly defined roles, access boundaries, human agency, accountability, domain practices, operational constraints, and independent oversight. Production-ready architectures should make agent behavior understandable, repeatable, and reviewable while addressing fragmented records, conflicting knowledge, unclear authority, and the political context of organizational information. Rizom, Varias, and Bots With an Attitude exemplify this durable theme across organizational coordination, embedded virtual team members, modular capabilities, and emergent behavior.

Keywords: embedded AI, autonomous agents, organizational workflows, virtual team members, human-AI collaboration, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, agent accountability, modular agents, institutional context
