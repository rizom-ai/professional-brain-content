---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI is the design and deployment of AI agents embedded in organizational, educational, social, and cultural contexts. Situated agents extend rather than replace human judgment by operating within named roles, bounded authority, explicit responsibilities, context-aware access, predictable behavior, and documented escalation and contestation procedures. Their outputs and actions should carry provenance and remain traceable so accountability can be assigned instead of laundered through automated systems. The framework treats AI deployment as an institutional design problem and a stress test for organizational weaknesses, including the erosion of ownership, shared judgment, and organizational memory when institutions offload decisions to systems operating with limited context. It supports virtual team members, workflow automation, modular and interoperable agents, shared knowledge infrastructure, and human-AI collaboration. Open standards, procurement requirements, open-source infrastructure, published design specifications, and documented real-world deployments can make accountable-agent practices implementable, verifiable, and reusable while preserving human autonomy and collective judgment. Keywords: embedded AI, institutional design, organizational roles, bounded authority, accountability, provenance, traceability, escalation, contestation, responsibility laundering, workflow automation, virtual team members, modular agents, interoperability, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, human-AI collaboration, open standards, procurement, open-source infrastructure, transactive memory
