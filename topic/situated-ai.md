---
title: Situated AI
---
Governed AI agent infrastructure covers the technical and institutional design of production-grade LLM-based agents embedded in organizational, educational, public-sector, and regulated environments. It includes self-hosted and vendor-hosted models, retrieval-augmented generation, organizational knowledge systems, interoperable agent protocols such as the Model Context Protocol, and modular workflow automation. The topic emphasizes the transition from experimental pilots to reliable operational systems through bounded authority, explicit roles, provenance, traceability, predictable behavior, escalation and contestation procedures, and human oversight. It also addresses regulatory and public-sector constraints, sovereignty, procurement, licensing, sustainability, open standards, open-source infrastructure, responsibility allocation, overconfident outputs, organizational memory, and deployment risk. Reusable systems should be inspectable, verifiable, accountable, and designed to preserve human autonomy.
