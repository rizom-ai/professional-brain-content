---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI concerns the design and deployment of AI agents embedded within human organizations, institutional knowledge systems, and existing coordination practices. It includes agents that identify, preserve, retrieve, and coordinate distributed organizational knowledge; automate routine information-management tasks; and support modular workflows and virtual team structures while directing human effort toward higher-value work. The field spans open, governed infrastructure, reference implementations, interoperable standards such as the Model Context Protocol, retrieval-augmented generation, self-hosted and vendor-hosted models, and deployment tooling that supports portability and sovereignty. Reliable systems require adaptation to social and institutional context, durable organizational memory, bounded authority, explicit roles, provenance, traceability, predictable behavior, escalation and contestation procedures, human oversight, and clear responsibility. The emphasis is on public-interest, open-source, and sustainable infrastructure that preserves human autonomy and remains useful beyond individual products or funding cycles. Keywords include situated AI, organizational AI agents, institutional knowledge, organizational memory, AI agent infrastructure, governed AI, workflow automation, virtual team members, modular agents, open standards, Model Context Protocol, retrieval-augmented generation, portability, human oversight, provenance, traceability, sovereignty, and public-sector AI.
