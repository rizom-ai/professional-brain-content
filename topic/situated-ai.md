---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI is the design and deployment of accountable AI agents embedded in defined organizational roles, social environments, and institutional contexts. These agents extend human judgment rather than replace it, operating with named responsibilities, bounded authority, context-aware access, provenance-carrying outputs, predictable behavior, and explicit escalation and contestation paths. The approach treats accountability and preservation of human autonomy as engineering requirements, addressing responsibility laundering and quiet institutional substitution when fluent systems operate without context or gradually absorb organizational memory and judgment. It encompasses open-source infrastructure, design standards, reference implementations, interoperability, shared organizational memory, knowledge provenance, modular agent networks, and real-world deployments for human-AI collaboration. Applications include organizational knowledge coordination, virtual team members, workflow automation, and collective knowledge stewardship, with institutional evaluation used to assess effects on collective judgment and refine accountable-agent practices. Actions should remain traceable, reviewable, and connected to identifiable human or institutional authority, including when records are fragmented or conflicting.
