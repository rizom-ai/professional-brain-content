---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI embeds language models and autonomous agents within software products, organizations, and working environments, treating virtual team members as participants in practical operating models rather than standalone tools. These systems handle focused responsibilities such as routine coordination, status collection, repetitive questions, administrative work, and preservation of shared context, enabling teams to devote more capacity to higher-value work. Effective deployment depends on organizational memory, production-ready knowledge infrastructure, clearly defined roles, access boundaries, human agency, accountability, domain practices, operational constraints, and independent oversight. Reliable architectures make agent behavior understandable, repeatable, and reviewable while addressing fragmented records, unclear authority, conflicting knowledge, and the political context of organizational information. Varias exemplifies this approach through an AI productivity system built around narrow, responsibility-bearing virtual team members embedded directly in human workflows.
