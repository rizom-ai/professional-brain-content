---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI is the design and deployment of accountable AI agents within defined organizational roles, social environments, and institutional contexts. It treats accountability as an implementable system property: agents should have named roles, bounded authority, context-aware access, provenance-carrying outputs, explicit escalation rules, understandable and repeatable behavior, and a genuine process for affected people to review or contest decisions. The domain includes open-source infrastructure, reference implementations, interoperability standards, shared organizational memory, knowledge provenance, modular agent networks, and deployment practices for human-AI collaboration. Situated AI addresses responsibility laundering by making agent actions traceable, reviewable, and connected to identifiable human or institutional authority, even when records are fragmented or conflicting. Applications include organizational knowledge coordination, virtual team members, routine workflow automation, and collective knowledge stewardship. Keywords: embedded AI, autonomous agents, organizational workflows, virtual team members, human-AI collaboration, organizational memory, knowledge infrastructure, agent accountability, modular agents, institutional context, open-source infrastructure, provenance, interoperability, escalation rules, contestability, bounded authority, situated-agent standards
