---
title: Situated AI
---
Situated AI infrastructure concerns the open, governed design of production-grade AI agents embedded in organizational, educational, public-sector, and regulated environments. It spans reference implementations, deployment tooling, portability guarantees, interoperable standards such as the Model Context Protocol, retrieval-augmented generation, organizational knowledge systems, and modular workflow automation. The focus is on durable public-interest infrastructure rather than short-lived products: systems should remain usable beyond individual funding cycles through open-source implementations, public documentation, shared knowledge, transparent specifications, and ecosystem coordination. Reliable deployment requires bounded authority, explicit roles, provenance, traceability, predictable behavior, escalation and contestation procedures, human oversight, and clear allocation of responsibility. The topic also covers self-hosted and vendor-hosted models, sovereignty, procurement, licensing, regulatory compliance, sustainability, organizational memory, deployment risk, and the preservation of human autonomy.
